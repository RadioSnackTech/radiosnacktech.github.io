## Open Print

<img src="/images/uploads/open-print-2026-10-20.jpg" alt="Open Print" style="max-width: 100%">

**Tuesday, October 20, 4pm to 7pm**

Join us for an afternoon for low-cost printing with our riso, Mille-Feuille. If you've taken the Risograph class with us, print your work at cost of materials. If you haven't had a chance to get trained on the risograph yet, we'll help you for a small fee. First come, first served!
