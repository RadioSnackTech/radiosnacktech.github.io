## Radio Snack Cafe

Come enjoy a coffee and snack at Radio Snack Cafe!

<img src="/images/uploads/rs-cafe-sept.png" alt="Radio Snack Cafe" style="max-width: 100%">

Things you can do hanging out in the space:

- Bring your favourite craft project to work on
- Participate in "No screen Sunday" and make new friends
- Get scheming for your next art project
- Enjoy cheap coffee and snacks
- Read a zine from our zine library
- Use our tools like laser cutter and 3D printer (as a member)

Check the [events page](/events) or our [calendar](/calendar) for the next date and time.
