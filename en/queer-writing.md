## Queer Writing Group

<img src="/images/uploads/queer_writers_group.jpg" alt="Queer Writing Group" style="max-width: 100%">

**Every second Saturday 1pm to 4pm**

Come join Max and Nico's biweekly writers' group.  It is every other Saturday from 1-3pm at Radio Snack (916 Ontario St. East #130) :slight_smile:.  No prompts, no pressure, just some good ol' fashioned body doubling and community.  Let us know if you may be interested in joining.  And, yes, we are having a special spoopy edition on October 31st!
