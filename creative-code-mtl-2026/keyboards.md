In this workshop, we’ll turn a phrase, word, or idea into a custom keyboard.
I’ll show participants how to design a "fun keyboard", prepare files for
keeb.io, draw a keyboard matrix for hardwiring, make custom keycaps, and prepare
files for laser cutting.
