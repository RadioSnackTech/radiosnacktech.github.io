The Cajita Abierta is a hardware
and software platform for
learning physical computing
based on the ESP32
microcontroller, useful when you
need to add sensors to your
projects, control motors or midi
or cv instruments. The workshop
is open-ended, responding to
participants' interests for their
projects and their familiarity
with platformio, Arduino and
coding+electronics.	We'll cover
the installation process and
loading examples to get
participants started.
